# Large mixed requests

- Apply when the base skill's mixed-request trigger fires. Complexity, permissions, dependencies and unfinished handovers matter more than item count; an ordinary question plus one small fix need not require a formal inventory.

- Before work, inventory every item in the existing central tracker with a stable ID and original wording or exact source reference. Split compound requests into checkable subitems (cost explanation and provider replacement are separate); attach each constraint to its item. Follow `references/progress.md`. Use one inventory, not a plan, worker or round per subitem; group execution and research while keeping outcomes separately checkable.

- Infer authorization from the whole Ask and context, not grammar: “Can you fix this?” can request action; “Is switching providers feasible?” can request discussion. Preserve action requests and checkpoints; discussion of capabilities or ideas does not itself authorize implementation.

- Follow dependencies and owner priorities, not a fixed questions-first/tasks-first order. Answer decision questions before dependent work; continue independent authorized tasks while investigating other questions. Complete any required repair or investigation before its dependent conclusion; distinguish established facts from pending conclusions.

- Where useful, deliver established answers in a question-delivery Reply, then continue authorized tasks in a linked implementation round. A separate round is optional: small independent tasks and requested explanatory documents can finish alongside answers. Urgent or explicitly prioritized work follows the owner's requested order. Earlier chat answers and findings in RUN/WIP do not replace the complete saved Reply required by `references/closeout.md`.

- Track answer delivery separately from task implementation and verification. A parent item is complete only when all requested subitems are accounted for; answering its question does not complete its associated task. Keep each item's authorization, dependencies, state, answer/result location and next action current. Do not mark unfinished or blocked work complete merely because its status was explained.

- Before closing a question-delivery or handover round, account for every unfinished question and task in its Reply. Carry their original references, authorization, decisions, constraints, dependencies, states and next actions into the next Ask, linking the same tracker and preserving history through the existing notebook-writing protocol. Identify what this delivery completes and what remains unfinished. Closing does not itself start continuation: resume authorized work when the host can continue, and otherwise state what is waiting and what must happen next. Never imply that a worker or background process has started without evidence.

- Continue already-authorized work without asking again whether to start. Pause dependent work only at an actual requested checkpoint, a missing owner decision, or an existing required approval or safety boundary; continue independent authorized work. Never treat an idea or a tracker entry as permission to implement.

- Compare the inventory with the original Ask before execution and with the Reply before delivery; check compound clauses, constraints and pending items, not just counts. Organize the saved Reply in the owner's original request order, even when execution follows another order. Keep tracker states and answer/result links current even with `log-verbosity: off`; chat updates remain available, and the complete saved Reply is still required.
