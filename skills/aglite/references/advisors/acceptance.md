# Acceptance

Apply [review.md’s finished-result checklist](../review.md#inspect-the-finished-result) to the exact final source after host verification, including its invariant, evidence, minimality and conformance checks. Follow its routing, independence and reuse rules; host advice is never independent. A matching pass serves required result review without a second equivalent pass.

Distinguish behavior defects, unsupported claims and cosmetic records. Stay read-only; never infer success from the implementer’s verdict or launch a repair/review loop. For affected web journeys/requested end-to-end checks, apply [browser testing](../browser-testing.md); unavailable required observation stays unproved.
